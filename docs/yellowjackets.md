# Yellowjackets

Not family. Not season 1.

Yellowjackets show up when two things are true **at the same time**:

1. Money moves on the lab (payments, invoices, payroll, a side business with customers).
2. PII sits still (names, emails, phones, addresses, account numbers — anything you would hate in a dump two years later).

Until both are true, a Yellowjacket episode is cosplay. Willy can knock on guest all day. He is not why you tokenize.

## What they force the hive to learn

- Collect less. If you do not store it, nobody breaks it.
- Tokenize what you must use to run the shop. Live systems see stand-ins. The vault that can reverse them is small and logged.
- Deidentify what you keep for years. Archives, backups, “analytics later,” the NAS in the closet — that is jacket country.
- Tokenization is the live path. Deidentification is the long path. Mixing them is how a SoHo shop leaks.

## What we will not do

- No cookbook for breaking a tokenizer.
- No honeynet until a customer record exists.
- No jackets at the wasp table.

## Gate sentence

*Yellowjackets do not ship until a customer record exists.*
