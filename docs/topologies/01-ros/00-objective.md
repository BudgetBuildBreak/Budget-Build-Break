# 01 Desk — objective

Prove that a carrier gateway, a cheap managed switch, and one used sensor box can produce a ticket a junior analyst could hand off.

Success for this fabric:

- Viewer can name each box's job in one sentence
- BOM has a price *and* a do-not-buy list
- 1B has a healthy Wazuh screenshot
- 1C has a filled ticket with one host/identity IoC and one network IoC
- AAR names the gap that creates topology 02

Out of scope: MDM herd, till, Yellowjackets, owning the SIM.
