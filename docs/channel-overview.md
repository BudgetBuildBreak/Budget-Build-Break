# Channel overview

**Budget. Build. Break.** is a SoHo networking and security channel.

Tagline: *Budget the build. Secure the network. Then we break it.*

YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1IUhkhpBqSj5mL0t13OZow

## Audience

**ThreeBees.** People standing up a home lab, a small office, or a side-business network who refuse to buy enterprise gear they do not need — and who want to see how that network fails the way a household next door would actually poke it.

## Food chain

- ThreeBees build the hive and the honey.
- Wasps (Willy, Wanda, the kids) are the yard. Classic weekend kit and this year's toy. They arrive as a family.
- Yellowjackets are not cousins. They show up when money moves and PII sits in long-term storage.

## Episode shape

See [episode-machine.md](episode-machine.md).

Short version: topology → weather → signature → triage → eradicate → rebuild → lessons learned.

The cookbook is defensive. Signatures and rebuilds live in the repo. The wasp kit is context, not a shopping tutorial.
