# Channel overview

**Budget. Build. Break.** is a SoHo networking and security channel.

Tagline: *Budget the build. Secure the network. Then we break it.*

YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1IUhkhpBqSj5mL0t13OZow

## Audience

People standing up a home lab, a small office, or a side-business network who refuse to buy enterprise gear they do not need — and who want to see how that network actually fails when it is attacked the way real threats attack it.

## Episode shape

Every video is one piece of a larger fabric:

1. **Why this piece exists** — the constraint or requirement that forced the choice.
2. **Where it maps** — how it sits in the overall design (WAN edge, core switch, wireless, identity, backup, monitoring).
3. **Break it** — what common threat signatures look like *on this exact build*.
