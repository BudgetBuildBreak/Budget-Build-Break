Fiction. The household next door. They did not build this line. They are on it anyway.

Wasps arrive as a family. Willy does not disappear because Lil Leroy got a toy.

## Rules

- Cartoon names only. Not a real neighbor. Not a real child.
- Show the object so the hive knows what “toy” means. Price class, one line, then turn the camera to the switch.
- Capture the signature on the topology in play. If it cannot be paused and screenshot, it did not happen.
- Classic and toy can share an episode. That is the layered night.
- Yellowjackets are not cousins. See [yellowjackets.md](yellowjackets.md).

## Language on camera

- “Willy is on the trunk.”
- “Wanda tried the labeled door.”
- “Leroy is a guest-shaped problem.”
- Never a street, unit number, or live SSID that is not the lab's.
