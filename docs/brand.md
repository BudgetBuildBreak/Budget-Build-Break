## System

Same geometry: block body, two-leaf wings, ball antennae, kick feet.
Different job: color and direction.

| Mark | Palette | Faces | Job | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **3Bee** | Gold head, gold/red/blue body, blue wings | Right (build) | Hive | Avatar, merch, end card |
| **Wasp** | Black head, red/black body, red wings | Left (weather) | Willy / Wanda / Leroy | 1C–4C title cards, threat-model header |
| **Yellowjacket** | Gold + black stripes, front view, sting | At you | Money + PII | Topology 05 only |

Gold on the 3Bee is *budget*. Gold on the jacket is *warning*. Do not mix those golds in one mark.

## Rules

1. Channel avatar is the 3Bee. Never the wasp.
2. Do not put “3B” inside the body. The three planes *are* the 3.
3. One-color stamp for cheap merch: navy for the bee, black for the wasp.
4. 32px: crop to the head. Full bug for thumbnails.
5. Do not paint the wasp yellow and call it a jacket. Jacket is a different silhouette (front, stripes, sting).
6. Circuit-trace wasp is retired.
