# ThreeBees

The hive. Builders. Viewers.

ThreeBees budget the box, map the hop, and stay for the rebuild. The bee is the audience mark. The wasp is the weather. Do not mix the merch.

## What a ThreeBee gets

- A topology they can copy on a budget
- A signature they can recognize on *this* fabric
- A playbook: triage, eradicate, rebuild, lessons learned

## What a ThreeBee does not get

- A recipe for the house next door
